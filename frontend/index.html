How SlopeShield picks a route

  1. Collect the facts

    Each of the 11 towns has its slope, elevation, soil type, rock strength, road condition and count of past landslides. Rainfall and humidity are added from weather data.

  2. Score the risk

    Terrain counts for 60% of the score and current weather for 40%. The result is a number from 0 to 100.

  3. Weigh every road

    The 14 roads form a graph. A risky road behaves like a longer road, and a critical road is blocked with a heavy penalty.

  4. Find the cheapest path

    Dijkstra's algorithm with a min-heap priority queue searches the graph twice: once for fewest kilometres, once for lowest risk.
